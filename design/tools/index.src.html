04

Things you might have missed

Not asked for, but each one is part of the redesign whichever direction wins. Each direction page has its own list too.

  • Inkling has no app icon yet. macOS 26 draws any icon that is not a full rounded square inside a grey tile, and asks for light, dark, clear and tinted versions. Each direction here ships light and dark; clear and tinted come with the build.
  • Every state needs words and a look: an empty note, nothing to ask, reading, offline, limit reached (with when it resets), signed out, and errors. Each direction page draws all of them.
  • Sound, off by default. One quiet sound per event, taken from the direction’s object: a pencil tick, a stamp, a thread pull. Try them with Sound on in the top bar.
  • Marks never rely on colour alone. A question and a correction differ in shape or carry a number, so they read in greyscale and for colour-blind eyes. VoiceOver gets labels like “Question 1 of 2, about 5:30”.
  • Selection colour, caret and focus ring belong to the direction too; today they are defaults.
  • Export and print use the direction’s typefaces and never print Inkling’s marks unless asked.
  • Open questions on the Dock icon as a badge, optional and off by default.
  • The walkthrough video comes after the pick, in the chosen direction’s motion and sound.
05

After you pick

  1. Build the look into the app.

    Tokens and bundled fonts in theme.rs; margin notes and tethers in margin.rs; anchors and the new gutter read marks in editor.rs; Inkling’s drawing ink in drawing.rs; the app icon through bundle.sh.

  2. Build the UX core.

    The small ones first (read marks, waiting while you type, open count and jump, undo, keys), then fix-the-words, Ask the room, the first run and the export appendix, then drawings as figures.

  3. Make the walkthrough video with sound, in the chosen direction’s motion and voice.

  4. Then Codex as a second provider, with its device sign-in.
06

The idea we didn’t take

The first idea anyone has for this product is a warm paper notebook, serif text, and the AI’s notes in a softer serif italic tied to dashed underlines. It is a good design. It is also riddled.ai, and it is today’s Inkling, whose palette was copied from it.

So each direction starts from a different object, and none of them uses a serif italic for Inkling’s voice. One idea runs under all three: Inkling lives in the dot of the i. Each wordmark replaces the dot of the first i with that direction’s mark (a caret, a highlighter square, a French knot), because an inkling is the smallest piece of ink there is.

If I hold 5:30 for the whole race, I’ll come in just under 50.
