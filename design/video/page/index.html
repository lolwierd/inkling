How it sounds

  • The rhythm is the needle: pierce clicks and cloth taps, and a sewing machine that speeds up and stops with the stitching on screen.
  • A bowed string rises as a thread pulls taut, and lets go into the knot.
  • A four-note motif stays unresolved while threads are open and comes home only when one is tied off.
  • Each mind has its own instrument, so a room of three sounds like three voices.

What is real, and what is staged

  • Real: every word Inkling and the other minds say, straight from the model through Inkling's brain.
  • Staged: the app is rebuilt in HTML rather than recorded; typing and model waits are sped up; the dictated words are typed by the film; the Obsidian vault and the email are made up; Copy and Make a note are only pointed at; and the border turning into darns at the end is a film device.
