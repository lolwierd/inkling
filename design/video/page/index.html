How it sounds

  • One quiet piece in F runs under the whole film: a felt piano over a bowed pad and a soft bass, slow, with room between the phrases.
  • Its one figure is a running stitch. Four notes go over and under a middle note, held and lifted in the proportion of the app's own stitches, and each phrase ends on its middle note, a knot.
  • The harmony moves where a chapter or a moment begins, and turns darker when the desk turns to Lamplight. There are no hits, and actions on screen make no sound.
  • Four moments are marked, and quietly: each tied-off thread lands a phrase on its knot with a low note under it, and the last knot becomes the dot of the i. While the threads are hidden, the melody rests.
  • It is mixed low and even, at -20 LUFS with peaks well under -3 dBTP.

What is real, and what is staged

  • Real: every word Inkling and the other minds say, straight from the model through Inkling's brain, each time with the note, its brief and its threads as the app would send them at that moment.
  • Real: the reads too. Three of the four paragraphs pass without a word because the model let them pass; the note was worded until it did.
  • Staged: the app is rebuilt in HTML rather than recorded; typing and model waits are sped up; the dictated words are typed by the film; the other notes, the Obsidian vault and the email are made up; Copy and Make a note are only pointed at; and the last knot becoming the dot of the i is a film device.
