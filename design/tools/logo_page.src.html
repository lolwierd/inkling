Why today's mark doesn't fit

  • It draws the tools, not the stitches. The icon is an illustration: a wooden hoop, a brass screw, a steel needle, wood and metal gradients. The app itself only ever draws thread on a flat page.
  • Its colour belongs to someone else. The tile is indigo, which in the app is another mind's thread. Inkling's own is madder, on linen or lamplight.
  • It falls apart small. At 32 px and below the hoop reads as a clock face and the stitched question mark disappears.
  • The knot sits on the word like a sticker. In the wordmark it is drawn 1.4 times the width of the dot it replaces (1.7 times in the app's sidebar), in three tones, so it reads as a swirl rather than a dot.

What every direction here keeps

  • Flat colour and the app's own stitch geometry from src/stitch.rs: capsule stitches, a knot with one lighter coil, tapered thread. An object keeps its materials (a reel is still wood) but is drawn flat, without the gradients and bevels of today's icon.
  • Madder for Inkling's thread; linen and lamplight for the ground. Other thread colours appear only where the object has more than one.
  • Letters are Fraunces Display (opsz 72, SOFT 100, WONK 0), outlined from the font the app bundles, so a wordmark matches its title bar exactly. Where the dot is a knot, it is drawn 1.16 times the dot's width.
  • Every icon is one full rounded square, so macOS 26 never puts it in a grey tile, and every one has a dark version.

The directions

Each one starts from a different object. Icons show light and dark; the wordmark follows the page's Linen or Lamplight switch. Shortlist the ones worth another round, and leave pointers in a direction's notes: the page keeps them and copies them out for chat.

Every mark here is generated, not hand-placed: design/tools/logo_directions.py draws them from the stitch geometry in src/stitch.rs and the palettes in src/theme.rs, outlines letters with outline.py from assets/fonts/FrauncesDisplay-Medium.ttf, and writes design/logo/<nn-name>/wordmark.svg, mark.svg, icon.svg and icon-dark.svg, then this page. Run it with uv run logo_directions.py from design/tools.

The wordmark and mark files follow the reader's light or dark setting on their own; the icons come as a light and a dark file, at 1024 px on the macOS grid (an 824 px rounded square). The clear and tinted variants macOS 26 asks for come with the build, from whichever direction is picked.
